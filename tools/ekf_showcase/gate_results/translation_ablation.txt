CAVEAT ON THE ABSOLUTE NUMBER. My "negatives" are heuristic -- articles with no Turkish
disaster or casualty word -- and that rule is wrong for some of them. "NATO: Russia's
soldier losses in Ukraine exceed 300,000" is, by the gate's OWN definition, a correct
admission: it states a toll for a group of people. My regex missed it because it looked
for deprem/oldu/yarali and not for war-casualty vocabulary.

So the 28% splits three ways, and only the third is a gate failure:
  1. correct admissions my labels got wrong   (Ukraine 300,000 losses)
  2. genuine hard negatives the descriptions do not cover
       "Heavy snow in Kars: 110 people stranded on road rescued"  -- a number, a rescue,
       no deaths; and conflict coverage with no toll at all
  3. true failures ("TRT provided journalism education to high school students")

What survives the caveat is the COMPARISON, because all three conditions score the same
articles under the same labels: translation does not help. The fix is the label
descriptions, and the examples say what v3 must decide -- whether a war casualty total
counts, and how to exclude a rescue or evacuation that carries a number but no deaths.
