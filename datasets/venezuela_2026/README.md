# Venezuela 2026 — real double-blind test for the EKF/MHT tracker

Paper 1's real-world capstone: run the pipeline (fine-tuned casualty extractor → EKF/gate
tracker) on the **real** 2026 Venezuela earthquake reporting stream and score its toll
estimate against the **official** figure trajectory. This is the model-mismatch test — the
extractor was fine-tuned on synthetic (sonnet-5) text; here it faces real news. See
[EKF_MHT_DESIGN.md](../../tools/events_working_papers/EKF_MHT_DESIGN.md) §12-13.

Event: magnitude 7.2 (near San Felipe) + 7.5 (near Yumare) earthquakes, 24 Jun 2026.

## ⚠ Copyright policy (strict)

Commit **only**: source URLs + timestamps (`sources.jsonl`), model-**extracted** observations
(`observations.jsonl`), and the official GT trajectory (`trajectory.jsonl`). **Never** commit
full article text. Article text is fetched at eval time into a **git-ignored** cache
(`data/venezuela_text_cache/`) and consumed by `ingest.py`; only the extracted figures persist.

## Layout

| file | role | committed |
|---|---|---|
| `sources.jsonl` | reporting stream: `{url, outlet, published, tier}` (no text) | yes |
| `observations.jsonl` | extracted obs: `{stream_id, t_hours, role, value, qualifier, source, url}` | yes (generated by `ingest.py`) |
| `trajectory.jsonl` | official GT toll over time (the answer key) | yes |
| `data/venezuela_text_cache/` | fetched article text, keyed by url hash | **no (gitignored)** |

`t_hours` origin = **2026-06-24 00:00 UTC** (mainshock day).

## Official GT trajectory (skeleton, V1)

Dead toll is the clean monotone signal; injured figures are inconsistent across officials
(recorded as reported); missing is only ever given as "thousands"/"tens of thousands"
(left null — a known GT limitation for that role).

| date | t_hours | dead | injured | source | who |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|---|
| 2026-06-25 | 24 | 188 | 1,520 | Al Jazeera | initial |
| 2026-06-26 | 42 | 235 | 4,300 | — | health minister |
| 2026-06-26 | 48 | 920 | 3,360 | CNN | Nat. Assembly Pres. Rodríguez |
| 2026-06-29 | 120 | 1,719 | 5,034 | Al Jazeera | govt (15,866 homeless) |
| 2026-08-07 | 1056 | 6,100 | 61,000* | Wikipedia | *hospitalized (metric shift) |

USGS PAGER predicted a possible >10,000 — treated as a forecast bound, **not** GT.
